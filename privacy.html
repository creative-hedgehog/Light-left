Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

What Light Left keeps

Your chosen place, your alarms and the people you add are saved only in this browser, on this device. They are never sent to us. We have no accounts and no database.

What we don’t do

No tracking, no advertising, no analytics cookies. Sun, moon and star positions are worked out on your device.

Fonts

The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when the page first opens. After that they’re kept on your device.

Deleting your data

Remove Light Left from your home screen, or clear this website’s data in your browser settings, and everything is gone.
